# September 5 fixes and validation pilots

This follow-up implements the TTNC masking and evaluation-average fixes, crosses
label-subset and probe seeds, adds structured history corruption, and tests a
retrieval-assisted decoder. All experiments use frozen validation, never test.
Artifacts are in `experiments/followup_20260905/`.

## Correctness changes

- Unknown TTNC categories now map to `<UNK>` (1), preserving the claim-valid mask.
  Padding remains 0. The raw parquet has 52 unknown TTNC tokens after removing
  trailing timing tokens, before minimum-history filtering; this is not a count
  of affected evaluation patients.
- New evaluation averages copy the trained online weights at batch 32, then
  update every batch with decay 0.9 (about 6.6 batches per half-life). This is an
  evaluation-only EMA, historically named Polyak in the code. Old checkpoints
  retain their saved weights and configuration; retrain to obtain a corrected
  average. The old seed-42 four-epoch checkpoint had only three updates at decay
  0.9999, leaving a 0.99970003 coefficient on initialization.
- Warmup, first-copy behavior, subsequent averaging, restoration of online
  weights, and unseen-TTNC claim validity have regression coverage.

The four-epoch seed-42 comparison uses the same current evaluation pipeline:

| Checkpoint / weights | MAE ($) | RMSE ($) | TTNC retrieval@5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original / historical average | 1,659.58 | 2,642.68 | .7732 |
| Original / online | 1,680.10 | 2,655.51 | .7528 |
| Corrected / average | 1,679.36 | 2,656.53 | .7550 |
| Corrected / online | 1,680.11 | 2,655.46 | .7522 |

The training outcome is effectively preserved; the fix changes what the
evaluation average represents. It does **not** improve cost metrics over the
historical, initialization-dominated average. A trained average now tracks its
online counterpart closely. `scripts/evaluate_representations.py` accepts
`--evaluation-weights online` for an explicit matched comparison.

Artifacts: `corrected_average_seed42_e4/`, `corrected_online.json`,
`legacy_online.json`, and `legacy_average.json`.

## Crossed label efficiency

Two existing online LeVJEPA encoders (42, 43), five label-subset seeds
(101--105), and two probe seeds (201, 202) were crossed on the same validation
split. Every representation sees the same labeled rows for a given label seed.
Raw baselines are fit once per label/probe pair, and full-label fits are not
duplicated across meaningless label seeds. Exact labeled positions are saved.

| Boosted input | 1% precision / AP | 5% | 100% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw | .071 / .066 | .079 / .062 | .188 / .135 |
| LeVJEPA | .065 / .045 | .121 / .094 | .177 / .157 |
| Raw + LeVJEPA | .062 / .046 | .110 / .092 | .219 / .162 |

The earlier doubling of precision at 1% labels does **not** replicate here.
The 5% and full-label fusion advantages remain in this pilot. Do not treat the
crossed runs as independent patients or their sample SD as a confidence interval.
Only two encoder seeds are included; the new result establishes sensitivity to
label selection, not a universal reversal of the pretraining benefit.
The paired raw+LeVJEPA minus raw precision deltas are -.0083 at 1%, +.0312 at
5%, and +.0312 at full labels.

Artifact: `crossed_labels/summary.json`.

## Retrieval-assisted generation

The first matched pilot uses the frozen seed-42 online LeVJEPA encoder, decoder
seed 201, all training labels, 20 decoder epochs, and hidden width 256. Cosine
retrieval finds 16 training contexts. Their next-claim codes and the patient's
last observed claim form at most 128 candidate tokens per modality. Training
retrieval excludes its own row and effectively identical context vectors;
validation retrieval has no access to validation target codes. The reference
bank is fixed to training examples. Candidate recall is scored only afterward.

A shared token-conditioned head scores just these candidate slots, with the same
code-copy and timing-copy boosts as the flat residual control. The models share
data, seed, hidden width and epochs, but are not parameter-count matched.

| Decoder | CPT AP | ICD AP | CPT F1 | ICD F1 | TTNC accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Copy only | .261 | .511 | .510 | **.714** | .550 |
| Flat copy + residual | .471 | .527 | .506 | .590 | .587 |
| Flat + candidate filter | .464 | .530 | .508 | .594 | .587 |
| Token-conditioned candidates | **.477** | **.625** | **.515** | .638 | **.591** |

Candidate recall is .698 CPT / .821 ICD. The ICD AP gain survives the filter-only
control, but copy-only still wins ICD F1 and exact-set accuracy. This supports
further investigation of ranking and calibration, not promotion as a superior
claim-set generator. Replication across encoder/decoder seeds is still needed.

Follow-up completed: the [nine-run replication](retrieval_replication_20260905.md)
retains the ranking gains across all seed pairs. Inner-training calibration
improves CPT sets over every control, while ICD sets remain tied with copy-only.
New-code candidate recall is only about 40%, and calibrated ICD addition recall
is 0.24%; the stronger total recall figures above are dominated by persistence.

Canonical artifact: `retrieval_generation_matched/summary.json`. The preliminary
`retrieval_generation/` pilot omitted timing-copy bias in the candidate head and
is retained as exploratory history, not the matched comparison.

## Structured missing history

The seed-42 cap-32 and LeVJEPA comparison fits each tail head once on complete
training history. Validation corruptions are contiguous claim blocks, the latest
context suffix, CPT-only loss, and ICD-only loss, at 0%, 30%, and 70% nominal
severity. Each mode uses the same perturbation seed across encoders.

Contiguous/latest deletion retains at least one context claim, so the model
cannot shift its held-out future boundary. A sole context claim is therefore
protected. With no event dates, a contiguous block means consecutive observed
claims, not a fixed calendar interval. Modality loss preserves timing and the
other modality. These are different failure modes from independent older-claim
dropout with a protected latest claim.

Per-mode results are in `robustness_{contiguous,latest,cpt,icd}/summary.json`.

| Corruption at 70% nominal severity | Cap-32 boosted precision / AP | LeVJEPA boosted precision / AP |
|---|---:|---:|
| None | .167 / .148 | .208 / .190 |
| Contiguous block | .167 / .109 | .188 / .126 |
| Latest context suffix | .208 / .126 | .188 / .143 |
| CPT modality | .104 / .056 | .125 / .083 |
| ICD modality | .104 / .055 | .188 / .112 |

LeVJEPA retains higher AP in all four structured conditions, but its complete
history advantage does not imply unchanged robustness: CPT loss sharply hurts
both precision and AP. Cutoff precision sometimes improves for cap-32 even as
AP worsens. These are single-encoder-seed, single-corruption-seed pilots, not
replicated robustness guarantees.

## Reproduction

Use the existing data contract `artifacts/data_contracts/claims_seed42_v1.json`
and the original data path. All scripts accept `--accelerator gpu` or `cpu`.

- `scripts/run_experiment.py`: recipe `levjepa_patient_views`, seed 42,
  `--representation-pretrain-epochs 4 --max-samples 20000 --max-claims 10000`.
- `scripts/run_tail_label_efficiency.py`: pass both LeVJEPA checkpoints as
  `levjepa_seed42=PATH` / `levjepa_seed43=PATH`,
  `--label-fractions 0.01 0.05 1.0 --label-seeds 101 102 103 104 105 --probe-seeds 201 202`.
- `scripts/run_retrieval_generation_probe.py`: seed-42 LeVJEPA checkpoint;
  defaults reproduce the matched decoder budgets above.
- `scripts/run_missing_history_robustness.py`: cap-32 and LeVJEPA seed-42
  checkpoints, `--drop-ratios 0 0.3 0.7`, one invocation per
  `--corruption contiguous`, `latest`, `cpt`, or `icd`.

Each invocation needs `--data-path`, `--data-contract`, and a fresh `--output-dir`.
The training runner also requires a unique `--name`. Large checkpoints are local
artifacts; JSON summaries contain the validation results and protocol metadata.
